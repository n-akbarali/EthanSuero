COMMON QUESTIONS (07 — FAQ)

What services do you specialize in? +

End-to-end digital brand experiences: brand strategy, visual identity, UI/UX design, motion design, and full-scale Webflow & front-end development.

What is a typical project timeline? +

Comprehensive design and development projects take between 4 to 8 weeks depending on scope, page count, and custom motion requirements.

How much does a typical project cost? +

Full website design and build engagements typically start at $10,000, with custom monthly retainers available for scaling product teams.

Do you work with early-stage startups? +

Yes! I partner with ambitious Seed and Series-A startups looking to make a bold market debut as well as established global enterprises.

Can you collaborate with our developers? +

Absolutely. I deliver tokenized design systems, interactive prototypes, and production-ready CSS/HTML documentation for seamless handoffs.

How do we kick off a new project? +

Click "Start a Project" to schedule a 20-minute discovery call. You will receive a tailored scope, timeline, and proposal within 48 hours.
